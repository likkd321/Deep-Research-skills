**Conclusion:** the safest first-game combination is **PVE co-op × not frame-sensitive × no host migration × 2–4 players (cap at 4–6)**. Four conditions go with it:
- The game is playable solo.
- Friend invites and room codes are the main way in; the public lobby list is secondary.
- All traffic is forced through Steam's relay network (SDR).
- Godot and GodotSteam stay pinned to fixed versions.
- When the host leaves, everyone must be sent back to the lobby cleanly.
- Of the 24 cells, only "PVE × not frame-sensitive × no migration × 4–6" is "feasible, high confidence".
- Every frame-sensitive PVP cell and every seamless-migration cell is "not recommended".

**Most useful first-hand evidence:**
- **WEBFISHING patch notes** (Godot 3.5 + GodotSteam, Steam News API). Within about a month of its October 2024 launch it shipped six or more networking patches:
  - Past 50 active lobbies, players could not find servers or join by code, which looked like region locking.
  - Room codes failed for lobbies that were too far away. Steam's lobby search defaults to the same or nearby regions only.
  - Outdated-version servers were changed to show in the list but be unjoinable.
  - Desync fixes, plus kick, ban, block and manual join approval were added.
- **Buckshot Roulette** (Godot):
  - On 2024-12-10, when public lobby search launched, the developers said connection issues were "not on our end, and we can't fix it" and asked players to port-forward UDP 4379/4380. That points to traffic not being forced through SDR; the networking stack itself is not confirmed.
  - It patched several soft-locks and crashes triggered when a player disconnects at the wrong moment.
- **Dome Keeper multiplayer** (Godot, online up to 8):
  - It has no public lobbies, only room codes and Steam friends.
  - Patches v5.0.1–v5.0.8 in 2026 are mostly reconnect state loss, sessions wrongly showing as full, and joins at game start causing crashes.
- **Upgrade cadence:**
  - **GodotSteam:** it released roughly 9 versions between 2026-07 and 2026-11, about one every two weeks, almost all with renamed or removed APIs (4.17, 4.21, 4.23.2). It also fixed a crash when a lobby member leaves (4.20) and the peer ID claim issue (4.24).
  - **Godot:** 4.6 and 4.7 are fully supported, 4.5 gets only security and platform fixes, and 4.4 and earlier are end of life.

**Commercial and cold-start data** (all third-party estimates):
- Gamalytic, 2025 releases: about 8% grossed over $100k, about 40% earned under $100, and 65.9% under $1,000.
- Games-Stats, Q1 2026: median revenue about $290.
- VG Insights: co-op is 5–6% of releases but 36–46% of copies sold.
- Alinea: co-op earned $4.1B on Steam in H1 2025.
- GameDiscoverCo: multiplayer games settle at about 6% of launch peak concurrent players.
- Joost van Dongen: 40 concurrent players is decent for an indie. With 3 modes a 4-player match waits about 7 minutes, and about 2:15 with 1 mode.
